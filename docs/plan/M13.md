## 2. Loupe and automatic snapping for Measure (M13-02, D-68)

- The magnifier of manual alignment (M12-13, `compare/align/AlignPickLayer.tsx`) becomes one shared
  loupe component, used by alignment unchanged and by Distance, Perimeter, Area, Calibrate and
  drawing a Scale Viewport: the spot under the pointer 4× larger, a crosshair, the snap marker and
  the snap kind. It flips sides near the window edges, hides off the page; Alt held means no snap
  for that click, as today.
- Picking a Measure tool or Calibrate turns snapping on (a short status note says so). When the

  meanwhile (P3).
- A Loupe toggle in the Measure ribbon's Snap group (on by default, remembered on the device);
  Settings → Measure: loupe size (small, medium, large), magnification (2×, 4×, 8×), "Turn on
  snapping when measuring".
- The loupe draws through the Render worker, at most one request per frame: no main-thread long
  task (rule 1). Perf test: pointer sweep over corpus overlay pair 14 (dense A1) with the loupe on.
- Tests: unit tests of the snapping on/restore rules; e2e per tool; axe; screenshots.
- **M13-02·O1:** View → Loupe (the greyed-out `view.loupe` command) turns the same loupe on in any
  tool. **M13-02·O2:** Line, Arrow, Polyline and Callout comments get the loupe and snapping.

## 3. Thin Lines, Ctrl+5 (M13-03 spike S32, then M13-05, D-69)

Thin Lines draws every line of the page 1 device pixel wide at any zoom; it changes the screen
only, never the file, printing, exports, snapshots or the CRS.

- Toggles: View ribbon, Page Display group; the status bar button (item 4); Ctrl+5; Tell me. One
  setting for all documents, remembered on the device.
- Covers the page's own stroked lines, including those inside form XObjects. Comments keep their
  widths. Lines exported as filled outlines and text are unchanged (Help says so). Applies in every
  normal view and in Document A and B windows of a comparison; the overlay window keeps its own
  Thicken thin lines.
- **Spike S32 (M13-03)** in `spikes/s32-thin-lines/`: PDFium has no thin-line render flag, and the
  Render worker owns the authoritative document and its save (§3.1). Routes: (a) set stroke widths
  to zero around each tile render (`FPDFPageObj_SetStrokeWidth`, recursing into form objects) and
  restore them, proving that Ctrl+S, Save As and flatten output stay byte-identical; (b) a
  second, view-only load of the document (original bytes plus the tail) used only for thin-line
  tiles; (c) thin-line tiles from the Work worker. Measures on corpus overlay pairs 08, 14 and 16:
  tile time on an A0 sheet, memory, correctness, save bytes. The result goes into ADR-034 as an
  amendment before M13-05 starts; if no route passes, the owner gets the options.
- Ctrl+5: Chrome and Edge keep Ctrl+1 … Ctrl+8 for their own tabs in a normal window (as for
  Ctrl+1 and Ctrl+2 today); Ctrl+5 works in the installed app. M13-05 checks a normal tab and, if
  needed, adds Ctrl+Alt+5 as the alternate on the shortcut sheet (D-29).
- Icon: our own drawing in the Fluent Regular style, Filled when on (rule 15).
- Tests: a 3 pt line renders 1 device pixel wide with Thin Lines on; save bytes identical with it
  on; e2e of every toggle; perf of tiles with it on; screenshots.

## 5. Previous view and Next view in the status bar (M13-04, D-70)

- Two arrow buttons (Fluent Arrow Left / Arrow Right) right of the page buttons, after a divider;
  disabled when there is nowhere to go; tooltips name Alt+Left and Alt+Right.
- **One history for the window** (P2), replacing the per-document history of
  `view/viewHistory.ts`. Recorded: typed page number, Go to Page, bookmarks, links, search
  results, Zoom to Area, thumbnails, switching document tabs, opening a document. Not recorded:
  plain scrolling, Next Page and Previous Page. Closing a document removes its entries; a
  comparison tab is recorded as a tab only.
- Tests: unit tests of the history rules (test-first); e2e of page box, tab switch and back; axe;
  screenshots.
- **Owner addition (2026-11-24), in M13-04:** a measured Distance starts and ends with an
  **Open arrow** (was Butt), like a dimension line; Perimeter keeps plain ends. Found on the way:
  PDFium wrote an arrow head's turn on an exactly level or upright line as `8.74228e-08`, which is
  not a PDF number, so that head was never drawn (any line comment too). The Render worker now
  rewrites those numbers as plain decimals in the appearance after each line or polyline is drawn.
- **Owner bug (2026-11-24), in M13-04:** the "This document has N digital signatures" bar could
  not be closed, and the owner asked for the same on every bar of its kind. Each now has a close
  button (×, "Close this message", keyboard reachable) that closes it for that document or
  comparison while the window is open (nothing stored): the digital signatures bar (Show
  Signatures and Protect → Signatures still list them), the document restrictions bar (the
  restrictions still hold), the XFA form bar, and the notes above a text comparison (scans, OCR,
  pages of limited support, early results). Left as they are: the recovery bar ("Not now"
  already closes it), What's new (has its ×), and the alignment check on a comparison sheet (a
  decision: Line up by hand or It looks right).

### What a template holds

- The workbook (`.xlsx`), kept on this device only (IndexedDB), never sent anywhere and never in
  an error report (rule 10). `.xlsm` and `.xls` are refused with a plain message; parts ExcelJS
  cannot keep (from S33) are named in a warning at import.
- Sheet, start row (required), pre-drawn rows (detected, with override).
- Detail cells (optional, cell addresses on that sheet): document name (file name without .pdf),
  document number, revision, title, reviewer, review date, status scheme, comment count, page
  count; the optional file-name rule.
- Columns: every field with a tick and a column, typed as a number or a letter (shown as
  "3 (C)"); two fields on one column are flagged. Fields: Sr. No., Comment No. (Comments pane
  number), Page, Page label, Comment type, Comment text, Author, Discipline, Date created, Date
  modified, Status, All replies, First reply, Last reply, Last reply author, Snapshot, Comment ID
  (stable, for reading responses back later), Fixed text.
- Comment-type ticks, Ignore highlights (on by default), status words, snapshot height limit, one
  sheet per page.

### Exporting

Comment → Export CRS: template drop-down (Standard CRS and the project templates, last used
selected), open documents to include, detail values prefilled, "only what the Comments pane
shows", Ignore highlights, Excel or CSV (CSV: the same columns, no pictures). Sheet copies per
document and per page are named by document and page label, cut to Excel's 31-character limit,
without the characters Excel forbids, and kept unique. The workbook is built in the Work worker
with progress and Cancel (rules 1, 2); the save target is asked first; nothing leaves the device.

### Setting up

Import starts four steps beside a live preview of the sheet (first 60 rows; merged cells, borders,
fills and column widths as in Excel, parsed in the Work worker): 1 sheet and start row, 2 detail
cells, 3 columns, 4 which comments, status words and options. A click on a preview cell places the
field being set; the preview marks every placed field and the pre-drawn rows. The template
manager (from the export dialog and from Settings) lists templates with Edit, Duplicate, Rename,
Delete, Export template file and Import template file (one JSON file holding the workbook and the
settings, versioned, no new dependency).

## 8. Measuring by clicks, Distance as a dimension (M13-10, D-72)

The owner's words (2026-11-24): Distance should work as in CAD: click point 1, release, click
point 2, then move the mouse sideways to mark the dimension, as in an engineering drawing;
holding the button down while dragging is hard. Perimeter and Area the same way.

- **Distance:** click the first point, click the second (the value follows the pointer), move the
  pointer sideways: the dimension line follows it, parallel to the two points, with a leader line
  from each point to just past it (3 pt); click to place it. Enter or a double-click places it
  where it is; a click on the second point again (or Enter right away) gives a plain line with no
  offset; Esc drops it and keeps the tool. The earlier press-and-drag still gives the two points,
  then the offset as above. The value label sits over the middle of the dimension line.
- **Perimeter and Area** were already placed by clicks; they keep it: click each point, finish
  with a double-click or Enter, an area also with a click on its first corner; Esc drops it.
- The status bar names each tool's clicks while it is on.
- Snapping, the loupe, automatic snapping (M13-02, O1, O2) and Shift (45° steps) work for every
  click, the offset click too; Alt still means no snap for that click. The open arrows of a
  Distance (M13-04) sit at the ends of the dimension line.
- **In the file:** the Line annotation's `/L` stays on the two measured points and the offset is
  the standard leader line entries `/LL` (signed, positive is clockwise from the start of `/L`),
  `/LLE` (3) and, when set, `/LLO` (ISO 32000-1 §12.5.6.7), so the value and `/Measure` are
  unchanged and other viewers know where the dimension goes. PDFium's own appearance knows no
  leader lines, so the Render worker draws the appearance itself: PDFium's appearance of the
  dimension line (colour, width, dashes, endings) plus the two leader lines, in the comment's
  `/Rect`, which holds them. A file's Line with `/LL` (made here or elsewhere) reads back with its
  offset and keeps it when edited; a point dragged later keeps the offset.
- Tests: unit tests of the geometry and the `/LL` sign (TDD), the appearance on real PDFium
  through Ctrl+S and back; e2e for clicks, offset, Enter, Esc, the old drag, Perimeter and Area,
  snapping; Help (measuring topic and its pictures), What's new, the owner's hand check (§4 of
  `docs/acceptance/M13.md`).
