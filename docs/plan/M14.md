Source: the owner's message of 2026-11-25: two bugs (zooming during a Distance, Perimeter or Area
starts a new measurement; a Distance opened in another desktop PDF editor selects and deletes its
text apart from its arrows and lines), no way to select, edit, resize or delete a scale viewport,
and a fuller measuring toolset for engineers (length, polylength, perimeter, area, volume, cutout,
angle, radius, diameter, count) "without complicating UI and architecture too much". The owner
exported the same two dimensions from Triya PDF and from another desktop editor as FDF: the other
editor stores each one as a single `/Line` (`/IT /LineDimension`, `/Cap true`, `/CP /Inline`,
`/LL`, `/LLE`, the value in `/Contents` and `/RC`); Triya PDF 1.1.0 stores a `/Line` plus a
`/FreeText` tied to it by `/IRT` and `/RT /Group`, which other editors treat as two items.

## Purpose, users and scope

- **Review first, light takeoff second** (Q2). The users are engineers checking drawings; every
  tool must work well as a markup on the sheet. On top: totals by group, export, an optional
  summary on the sheet. Not a full estimating product.
- **One measurement is one standard PDF markup** (Q4) with a correct appearance and its value as
  its text. Other editors show it, select it, move it and delete it as one item; only Triya PDF
  edits it again as a measurement. No other product's private takeoff data is copied or matched.

## 1. Spike S34: every kind as one markup (M14-01, D-74)

The storage questions come first, so no tool is built on an assumption that fails in another
viewer (Q39). Work in `spikes/s34-measure-markups/`.

- **Measure corpus.** A generator in `e2e/fixtures/measure-corpus/` (not a folder named `corpus`,
  which `.gitignore` drops) writes self-made drawings into `Synthetic PDF Corpus/measure/`: a floor
  plan at 1:100 in mm, a pipe run with bends at 1:50, a sheet with a 1:50 main view and two detail
  viewports (1:5, 1:10), a sheet with no scale, a raster-only sheet, an imperial sheet in ft-in, a
  rotated page. Each with a manifest of true lengths, areas, angles and radii.
- **Captions in the appearance.** For each kind, the value drawn inside the markup's own
  appearance stream by the Render worker (as M13-10 already does for leader lines): Line with
  `/Cap true` and `/CP /Top` or `/Inline`; PolyLine and Polygon (`/IT /PolyLineDimension`,
  `/PolygonDimension`) with the value in `/Contents`; the kinds with no standard type (angle,
  radius, diameter, volume, cutout, count, the summary table) on the nearest standard subtype.
  The spike proposes the subtype for each (for example PolyLine of three points for an angle,
  Circle for radius and diameter, a small Square or Polygon for a count symbol, FreeText for the
  summary table) and records why.
- **Triya data.** What only Triya PDF needs to edit a measurement again (tool, label, count group
  and number, depth, holes, caption position, the arc of a partial circle) is kept in the page's
  `/PieceInfo` (ISO 32000-1 §14.5, the standard place for an application's own data, already used
  by the comparison file), keyed by each annotation's `/NM`, never as extra keys on the
  annotation. Distance, perimeter and area keep the standard `/Measure` data. A markup whose data
  is missing or no longer matches (edited elsewhere) still shows as a plain markup and is read
  back as well as its geometry allows.
- **Cutout holes.** The outer outline stays the Polygon's `/Vertices`; holes live in the Triya
  data and are cut out of the appearance with an even-odd fill. The spike checks how each viewer
  shows, moves and deletes such an area, and what happens when another editor redraws it.
- **Interop pack for the owner:** one file with every kind (and a v1.1.0 Distance for the update
  bar), checked by hand in Adobe Acrobat Reader, the second desktop editor the owner used, and the
  PDF viewer in Chrome or Edge: each markup looks right, selects as one item, moves and deletes as
  one item, its value can be read, and a moved markup keeps its appearance. Results go into
  ADR-035 as Amendment 1; if a kind cannot be one markup, that lane stops and the owner gets the
  options (CLAUDE.md workflow). **Owner gate.**
- Supersedes ADR-007 decision 2 (the grouped FreeText label): the value now lives in the markup.

## 2. Measuring that survives zoom and pan (M14-02, D-78)

- **Bug 1 (Q19):** while a Length, Polylength, Perimeter, Area, Volume, Cutout, Angle, Radius or
  Diameter is in progress, any view change keeps its points: zoom by wheel, keys or the zoom box,
  pan with Space-drag or the middle button, scroll, the loupe, Previous and Next view. The next
  click continues the same measurement, as Calibrate already does. Only Esc, another tool, or a
  change of page ends it; a page change says so in a short status note. No measurement runs across
  pages.
- **Shift (Q25):** locks the next point to horizontal, vertical or 45° from the last one and still
  snaps: the point lands where the locked direction meets a drawn line (today Shift turns snapping
  off, `annotate/tools/drawTools.ts`). Alt still means no snap for that click.
- **No scale yet (Q29):** the first measurement of a size on a page with no scale asks once: Set
  scale, Calibrate, or Measure at paper size. The answer is kept for that page for the session and
  with the document's scale data. Angle and Count never ask.
- **Two scale areas (Q30):** while drawing, the live value turns into a warning ("crosses Detail
  A") as soon as the pointer leaves the scale area the measurement started in. Today's rule stays
  for a placed one: no number, "spans 2 scales".
- Tests: unit tests of the gesture state across view changes (test-first) and of the Shift
  intersection; e2e on the measure corpus for each view change during each click tool; axe;
  screenshots of the prompt and the warning.

## 3. Length, Polylength, Perimeter and Area as one markup (M14-03, D-73, D-74)

- **Length replaces Distance (Q5).** Click, click, move sideways to set the dimension line, click
  (M13-10). A double-click on the second point or Enter places it with no offset. Stored as one
  Line (`/IT /LineDimension`, `/Cap true`, `/LL`, `/LLE`) with the value as its caption inside its
  own appearance; no separate text box. Caption position in the Properties pane (Q20): **Above**
  (default, `/CP /Top`) or In line (`/CP /Inline`, the line broken for the text).
- **Polylength and Perimeter (Q6).** Polylength is always an open path; Perimeter always closes
  back to the first point when finished, so the closing side counts. The value sits on the
  longest segment (Q40).
- **Area (Q3).** Straight edges only; the value in the middle of the shape (Q40).
- **Text on the sheet (Q27):** the value alone ("5,000 mm", "42.5 m²"); an optional Label in the
  Properties pane goes in front ("Slab S1: 42.5 m²"). The tool name is the markup's subject
  (`/Subj`), so other editors' comment lists say "Length", "Area". Captions can be dragged (Q40).
- **Ribbon (Q26):** the Measure tab gets four groups: Length (Length, Polylength, Perimeter), Area
  (Area, Volume, Cutout), Curves and angles (Angle, Radius, Diameter), Count. Each later task adds
  its own buttons (out-of-scope commands are omitted, §8). Home and Comment get one Measure split
  button: its face is the last measuring tool used, its menu holds every measuring tool.
- **Styles (Q31):** each tool remembers its own colour, line width, line ends and (Area, Volume)
  see-through fill. Defaults: today's red; open arrows on Length, butt ends on the others; a light
  see-through fill on areas. Text in the app's own font at 10 pt (Q40).
- **v1.1.0 files (Q38):** a file holding Distance measurements saved as a Line plus a grouped
  FreeText shows an information bar, "Update old measurements", with a close button like the other
  bars. One click turns each pair into one Line with its caption (one Undo step); nothing changes
  without that click.
- Tests: unit tests of caption placement and the v1.1.0 pair detection; the appearance through
  Ctrl+S and back on real PDFium; e2e per tool; the interop file from M14-01 rebuilt from the real
  tools; axe; screenshots.

## 4. Editing placed measurements; following scale changes (M14-04, D-78, D-77)

- **Point editing (Q24):** select a measurement and drag any point, or a Length's dimension line;
  the value updates while dragging. Right-click on an edge adds a point, right-click on a point
  removes it (Polylength, Perimeter, Area, Volume, cutout holes). Snapping, Shift and the loupe work
  while editing. Arrow keys move a selected measurement (Q36). One Undo step per edit (Q40).
- **Scale changes (Q23):** editing a viewport's scale, moving or resizing a viewport, or
  recalibrating a page updates the value and caption of every measurement that uses it, in one
  Undo step, with a note ("12 measurements updated") and Undo in it.
- Tests: unit tests of the add and remove rules and of which measurements a scale change touches;
  e2e; the appearance after an edit through save and reload.

## 5. Angle, Radius and Diameter (M14-05, D-73)

- **Angle (Q9):** three clicks: the end of the first arm, the corner, the end of the second arm.
  Measures the inside angle and draws its arc at the corner. Degrees, 1 decimal by default (Q40).
  Text "∠ 37.5°". Needs no scale.
- **Radius and Diameter (Q10):** two ways for both, chosen in the Properties pane and remembered:
  centre then a point on the edge, or three points anywhere on the circle or arc. The result is the
  circle (or only the arc, for a partial curve) with a radius or diameter line and the value: "R
  1,200 mm", "Ø 2,400 mm".
- Tests: unit tests of the three-point circle, the inside angle and their edge cases (points in a
  line, coincident points) test-first; e2e on the pipe-run sheet against its manifest.

## 6. Volume and Cutout; area and volume units (M14-06, D-73)

- **Volume (Q7):** an area outline times a Depth set in the Properties pane before drawing,
  remembered for the next volume and editable later (the value updates). The sheet shows the volume
  ("12.75 m³"); area and depth show in the Properties pane and the Measurements pane (Q40).
- **Cutout (Q8):** select an Area or Volume, press Cutout, click the outline of a hole inside it.
  The hole is a gap in the same markup and the value is net ("42.5 m²", with "3 cutouts" in the
  pane). Holes can be edited like points (M14-04) and deleted. A hole must lie inside the outline.
- **Units (Q28):** the length unit comes from the scale; Area and Volume have their own units and
  decimals in the Properties pane, saved with the document's scale data. Defaults: a length in mm
  or m gives m² and m³; in feet or ft-in gives sq ft and cu ft (cu yd as an option).
- Tests: unit tests of net area with holes and unit conversion (test-first); e2e on the floor plan.

## 7. Count (M14-07, D-75)

- **One symbol per click (Q11):** each click places its own small markup, tagged with its count
  group, with its number beside it inside the same markup ("Door 7"). The number can be switched
  off in the Properties pane.
- **Groups (Q14):** chosen or created before clicking, in a Count group box in the Properties pane:
  name, symbol shape (circle, square, triangle, diamond, cross, tick), colour (standard swatch
  picker), size. The last group used is remembered. Numbers start at 1 per group and run across
  every page of the document.
- **Deleting (Q15):** the others keep their numbers; the next new one takes the highest number plus
  one. "Renumber group" in the Measurements pane closes the gaps when asked (one Undo step).
- **Same-size numbers (Q12, Q13):** a view option, off by default, toggled from the status bar
  (the labelled toggle of D-70, beside Thin Lines), keeps count numbers the same size on screen at
  any zoom, for screen sharing. Small (about the status bar's text), on a see-through background,
  and capped: past about 4 times their saved size relative to the page they shrink with it. Screen
  only; the file, print and exports are unchanged.
- Tests: unit tests of numbering and renumbering (test-first); e2e; the view option at several
  zooms; screenshots.

## 8. The Measurements pane (M14-08, D-76)

- **Where (Q16):** a new pane in the left rail, next to Comments and Bookmarks.
- **What it shows:** measurements grouped by tool and by count group, each with its value, label,
  page and scale; a total per group; This page or All pages; a click on a row selects the markup
  and shows it. Flags: "spans 2 scales" (left out of totals, Q30), "paper size" (Q29).
- **Scales section (Q22):** every page's scale and viewports, with name and ratio; a click shows
  and selects the viewport. Replaces today's viewport list in the Properties pane (`ViewportList`).
- **Comments and CRS (Q34):** measurements stay in the Comments pane, with a Measurements filter
  to hide or show them; Export CRS gets "Include measurements", off by default.
- Totals and grouping are plain sums over values the workers already hand back; any work that
  reads the document runs in a worker (rule 1).
- Tests: unit tests of grouping and totals with units; e2e; keyboard path through the pane (Q36);
  axe light and dark; screenshots at 1280, 1366 and 1920 px.

## 9. Scale viewports on the page (M14-09, D-77)

- While the Measure tab is open (Q22): a click on a viewport's border selects it; drag to move;
  handles resize it; Delete removes it; a double-click opens its name and scale. On other tabs the
  borders take no clicks, so they never get in the way of commenting.
- Keyboard: the Scales section reaches each viewport; arrow keys move a selected one (Q36).
- Changes go through M14-04's scale-change rule (values follow, note and Undo).
- Tests: e2e of every action by mouse and keyboard; the `/VP` entries after save and reload.

## 10. Export, attachment and the summary on the sheet (M14-10, D-76)

- **Excel (Q32):** a fixed layout. Sheet 1 lists every measurement: No., Page, Scale area, Tool,
  Group or Label, Value, Unit, Depth, Scale, Author, Date, values as real numbers. Sheet 2 holds
  the totals per group. CSV gives the rows only. Built by ExcelJS in the Work worker with progress
  and Cancel (rules 1, 2); the save target is asked first. Export into project templates is later.
- **Pictures (Q33):** "Include pictures", remembered between exports. Each picture frames its
  measurement with a margin and at least the comment sheet's minimum surroundings (160 × 100 pt),
  so a count symbol shows what is around it; a large area fits whole; sized to its row. The CRS
  picture code is reused.
- **Attach to this PDF (Q17, Q18):** an option in the export dialog puts the Excel file in the
  document's attachments; attaching again replaces the earlier copy. When measurements change
  later, the Measurements pane says the attachment is out of date, with a button to refresh it.
  Never done by Ctrl+S.
- **Summary on the sheet (Q17):** "Place summary" puts one table markup on the page with each group
  and its total. Triya PDF keeps it up to date as measurements change; other viewers and prints
  see a normal markup.
- Tests: workbooks read back with ExcelJS in unit tests; e2e of export, attach, refresh and the
  summary; the owner opens a sample in Excel.

## Order of work

Two threads at a time, one per lane; when a task merges, the next eligible task in its lane
starts without asking (as in M12 and M13). Threads rebase and resolve conflicts themselves.

- **Lane 1 (markups and tools):** M14-01 (owner gate) → M14-03 → M14-04 → M14-05 → M14-06 →
  M14-07.
- **Lane 2 (interaction, pane, viewports, export):** M14-02 → M14-08 (once M14-03 has merged) →
  M14-09 → M14-10.
- **Then:** M14-11 when both lanes are done; the owner's hand check; the `v1.2.0` tag.

## Not in M14 (Q41)

Curved edges on Area (a later spike); another product's private takeoff format; saved style
presets; measurement export into project Excel templates; whole-page overview pictures in the
export; merging overlapping count numbers into bubbles; an angle from two picked lines; placing
points by keyboard or typing exact lengths; measurements across pages; Bluebeam Revu as a required
viewer; slope or pitch, dynamic fill, 3D.
