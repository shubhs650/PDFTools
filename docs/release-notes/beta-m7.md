# Triya PDF beta: what's new (November 2026, second update)

Thank you for your second review. **PDFTools is now Triya PDF.** The address, your settings,
styles and saved signatures stay as they were, and an installed app keeps working (the new name
shows after the next update). Everything still works offline, and your documents never leave
your device.

## Looks

- **Dark theme:** the moon button at the top right (or Ctrl+Shift+D, View → Dark Theme, or File
  → Settings → Appearance). Light stays the default; your choice is remembered on this device.
  Pages stay white.
- **The Home tab** is now Tools › Zoom › View › Comment › Measure › Protect. Tools (Hand,
  Select, Snapshot, Find, Advanced Search) and View (rotate the view, the side panes, the
  Properties pane) are icons; point at one for its name and shortcut. Protect has Sign Document;
  Reset Form is on the Form tab only.

## Text boxes, typewriters and callouts

- A new one is ready to type in, with "Type here" selected. **Esc or a click elsewhere keeps
  your text** (before, it could be lost).
- **Click and drag** a text comment to move it, including a callout by its box; double-click to
  edit where you clicked; Enter or F2 edits the selected one.
- **Draw a callout in one drag**, from the point it marks to where its box goes.
- **They grow with your text**: a text box or callout gets taller as you type, and a typewriter
  grows with its line, so nothing is cut off.
- **Right-click while editing** for Undo, Redo, Cut, Copy, Paste, Delete, Select All, Text Style
  and Properties.
- A text box and a callout have a **Text color and a Border color** of their own.
- The **typewriter** writes black Courier.

## Selecting comments

- **Drag a rectangle** on the page to select every comment it touches.
- **Shift+click** (or Ctrl+click) adds a comment to the selection or takes it out.
- A comment you have just drawn stays selected, ready for the Properties pane, Delete or the
  right-click menu.

## Sticky notes

- A sticky note opens a **window on the page**: several lines (Enter is a new line), the author
  and date, status, lock and Reply. Move and resize it; Ctrl+Enter or a click elsewhere keeps the
  text, Esc closes it. It is saved as a standard PDF pop-up, so other PDF viewers can show it too.

## More

- **Highlights** (Highlight, Highlight Area, Free Highlight) are 50 % see-through by default.
- **Paste a picture** (a screenshot, or an image copied elsewhere) onto the page with Ctrl+V or
  the right-click Paste; it is scaled to fit the page.
- **Form → Highlight Fields** now tints check boxes and radio buttons too.
- Help → Help has new sections on all of this.

## Good to know

- A text comment has one style for all of its text (no styles for single words).
- Comments made here are standard PDF objects. We are checking them in Adobe Acrobat Reader and
  PDF-XChange; if something looks different there, please report it with the Report an Issue on GitHub button at the top right.
