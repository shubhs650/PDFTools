# Triya PDF 1.1.0

Compare revisions. Engineers get new revisions of drawings and documents all the time, and the
question is always the same: what changed? Version 1.1.0 answers it on your own device, offline,
with no upload and no AI: a **PDF overlay** for drawings and a **text comparison** for documents.

Start from the new **Compare** tab, from **Compare with…** on a document tab's menu, or by
dragging one document tab onto another and holding it there.

## PDF overlay (drawings)

- **Old in red, new in green, the same in black.** Each revision is drawn without its comments,
  tinted and multiplied, at any zoom. Red and blue, magenta and green (safe for colour-blind
  readers) or your own colours.
- **Overlay controls:** show or hide each revision, a balance slider, Fade unchanged, Thicken
  thin lines, and Blink (hold X) to flip between old and new. Comments of each document on top
  with their own switches, Hide check highlights, and the layers of each file.
- **Pages paired for you,** including added, removed and reordered sheets, in an editable
  pairing table. Each side takes one file or a set of files.
- **Aligned for you** when a sheet moved, was rescaled or rotated, with the shift, scale and
  rotation shown. Or align by one to three point pairs, with snapping and a magnifier, numeric
  fine-tuning and arrow-key nudges.
- **Every difference listed:** added, removed or changed, with the old and new text where text
  changed ("Ø120 → Ø128"). Next and previous with F8 and Shift+F8, a checked tick, filters and
  search, sensitivity and a minimum size applied live, ignore zones for title blocks and revision
  tables (saved as named sets), percent changed per sheet, and Make cloud comments.
- **Compare an area:** a detail that moved to another place or another sheet, overlaid through
  its own alignment.
- **Vector overlay (beta):** see the sheet the way the exported overlay PDF draws it.

## Text comparison (documents)

- **Side by side,** scrolled together by content, with bands joining each change.
- **Marks in colour and shape:** deleted (red, struck through), inserted (green, underlined),
  replaced (amber), moved (blue, with an edit inside a move still shown), and changed figures.
- **Engineering values kept whole:** 1,450.00 mm, P-101A, 2026-11-22; a changed number shows its
  percent change. Table rows compared as rows.
- **Experimental:** headers and footers left out (and a changed one reported once), with bands
  you can draw; clause renumbering reported once, with references that did not follow flagged.
- **Scans** read by OCR for the comparison only, with "From OCR" and "Possible OCR error" labels.
  Every script, including right-to-left text and languages without spaces.

## Review across revisions

- **Three live windows** in the Review layout: the overlay, Document A and Document B, kept on
  the same spot, with every comment tool, the Properties pane and every right-click menu.
- **Close-out list:** go through the old revision's comments and mark each Addressed, Carried
  Over (copied to the new revision) or Changed Differently. The old comment is set to Completed
  with a reply that says what happened, so other PDF viewers and the comment exports show it.
- **Copy to New Revision** (and back) puts a comment at the matching spot of the other revision.

## Saving and outputs

- **Save review (Ctrl+S)** saves your new comments into Document A and Document B, as a normal
  incremental save of each file.
- **Save Comparison…** writes one standard PDF that any viewer opens: the overlay pages (with an
  optional summary page) or the new revision with every change marked, with the documents
  attached unchanged. It reopens in Triya PDF as the live comparison. The dialog says plainly
  that the file contains both documents.
- **Export Overlay…** (vector PDF), **Export Change Register…** (Excel or CSV, following the
  list's filters), and **Save as Preset…** for settings you use again.

## Try it with samples

- **Try a sample** under the two sides of the input window fills Document A and Document B with
  one of two sample pairs for each kind of comparison (a flange drawing and a checked print with
  review comments; an EPC tender and a commercial invoice), with a line on what to look for.
  With no document open, the start screen offers **Try comparing two revisions**.

## More for drawings

- **A loupe beside the pointer** while you measure (Distance, Perimeter, Area, Calibrate, Scale
  Viewport) or draw a line, arrow, polyline or callout: the spot under the pointer larger, with
  the point it will snap to and its kind. Press **M** to show or hide it; **View › Loupe** shows
  it with any tool. Size and magnification in File › Settings › Measure.
- **Snapping turns itself on** when you pick a measuring tool, and goes back when you finish.
- **Measure by clicks, as in CAD.** Distance: click the first point, click the second, move
  the pointer sideways to set the dimension line, and click to place it, with a leader line from
  each point and open arrows at its ends. Enter places it where it is; Esc drops it. Perimeter
  and Area: click each point, then double-click or press Enter (an area also closes on its first
  corner). The dimension line's place is saved as the standard leader line entries of the PDF
  comment, so other PDF viewers draw it the same way.
- **Thin Lines** (Ctrl+Alt+5, or Ctrl+5 in the installed app; also in the status bar and View ›
  Page Display) draws every line of the page one pixel wide on screen, at any zoom. Files,
  printing and exports are unchanged.

## Moving around

- **Previous View and Next View** (Alt+Left, Alt+Right) in the status bar, with one history for
  the window: typed pages, bookmarks, links, search results, thumbnails, Zoom to Area and tab
  switches are steps.
- **Labelled status bar buttons** for Thin Lines and Enable Snapping, and information bars (for
  example about signatures or restrictions) can be closed.

## Comment review sheets in your project's Excel form

- **Comment › Export CRS (Excel)** can fill your project's own Excel form: import the empty form
  once, and four short steps beside a live preview of the sheet show where the comments go
  (click a cell to place each field; suggestions are read from the headings).
- Rows already drawn are filled first, then rows are added with the start row's format,
  formulas, drop-down lists and conditional formatting. Document number, revision and the like
  go in their own cells, optionally read from the file name.
- **Several open documents** in one workbook, one sheet each; optionally one sheet per page.
- Templates stay on this device; share one with a colleague as a template file.

## Also new

- Help has a new chapter, **Compare revisions**, and the comparison windows have "?" buttons
  that open it at the right topic. New topics for the samples, the loupe, Thin Lines, Previous
  and Next View, and CRS project templates, and every Help picture is new.
- The keyboard shortcut sheet lists every key, including the keys that work in one pane or
  window (F8 in a comparison, hold X to blink, M for the loupe).
