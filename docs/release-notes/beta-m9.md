# Triya PDF beta: what's new (November 2026, Help)

**Help now opens in its own window**, with step-by-step instructions and screenshots of the real
app. It replaces the small Help box. Like the rest of Triya PDF, it works offline, and nothing
about your documents is sent to it.

## Opening Help

- **F1**, or **Help › Help**, opens Help at the topic for what you are doing: the chapter of the
  ribbon tab you are on, or the dialog that is open.
- The main dialogs (Sign Document, Password Security, Security Info, Comment Styles, Flatten
  Comments, Import and Export Comments, OCR, Print, Save Optimized, Set Scale, Stamps, Apply
  Redactions, Send Error Report and more) have a **?** button next to Close that opens their
  topic.
- Help stays in one window: pressing F1 again brings it forward at the new topic.

## In the Help window

- **43 topics in 11 chapters**, from opening files to redaction, each written as numbered steps.
- **51 screenshots** with numbered markers. Click one to see it full size.
- **Search** with Enter (every word must match), with a short extract for each result.
- **Print** a topic, or **Print all topics** as a manual.
- The keys shown match your computer (Ctrl on Windows, Cmd on a Mac) and the light or dark theme
  follows the app.
