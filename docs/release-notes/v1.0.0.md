# Triya PDF 1.0.0

The first public release of Triya PDF: a free, offline, private PDF editor that runs in your
browser, made for engineers who work with drawings, specifications and contracts.

Open it at **https://app.triyapdf.com** or **https://triya-pdf.openengtools.com**. Nothing to
install, no account. To use it like a desktop app, choose **Install** in Chrome or Edge's
address bar.

## Highlights

- **Your files stay with you.** Documents are opened, rendered and saved in your browser and
  never uploaded. No account, no cookies, no analytics. After the first visit the whole app
  works offline.
- **Fast on big files.** Work runs in background workers, so the window stays responsive on
  large drawings and long documents.
- **Standard PDFs out.** Comments, form fields, bookmarks and signatures are standard PDF
  objects, so Acrobat Reader and other PDF viewers show them. Ctrl+S saves only what changed.

## What you can do

- **View and find:** document tabs, thumbnails, bookmarks, layers, attachments; zoom, rotate and
  page layouts; Find and Advanced Search (whole words, match case, all open files); print with a
  print-ready option.
- **Comment and mark up:** highlight, underline, strike out, sticky notes with replies, text
  boxes, typewriter and callouts with a font library that ships with the app, lines, arrows,
  rectangles, ovals, polygons, clouds, pencil, stamps (including your own images), comment
  styles, and a Comments pane to filter, review and set status.
- **Measure:** set or calibrate a scale (also per viewport), then measure distance, perimeter and
  area with snapping to endpoints, midpoints, intersections and the nearest line.
- **Organize:** insert, delete, move, rotate, extract, split, crop and merge pages; images to PDF;
  create a blank PDF.
- **Forms:** fill and save forms; create text fields, check boxes, radio buttons, lists and
  dropdowns.
- **Convert:** export text, Markdown, HTML, Word and images; extract tables to Excel;
  recognise text in scans (OCR) so it can be searched and selected.
- **Protect:** redact text and areas for good (a new, fully rewritten and verified file);
  password protection and removal; visual signatures; an offline integrity check of digital
  signatures.
- **Comments in and out:** export and import comments as JSON, and copy comments from one
  revision of a drawing to the next.
- **Your way:** light and dark theme, Compact or Comfortable density, an adaptive ribbon, Tell me
  (Alt+Q) to find any command, full keyboard access and screen-reader names, and Help that works
  offline.

## Known limits

- Certificate-based digital signing is not available yet; visual signatures are.
- English only for now.
