# Triya PDF 1.2.0

Measuring, rebuilt for engineers. Version 1.2.0 turns measuring from three tools into a full
review and light takeoff kit: ten tools, each measurement one standard PDF comment that other
viewers handle as one item, a pane with totals, and export to Excel. Everything still runs on
your own device, offline.

## Ten tools on the Measure tab

- **Length** (replaces Distance): two clicks, then move the pointer sideways to set the
  dimension line and click to place it, or press Enter to place it on the line. Its caption sits
  above the line or in it.
- **Polylength** for an open path such as a pipe run, and **Perimeter**, which always closes
  back to the first point so the closing side counts.
- **Area** with straight edges; **Volume**, an area times a depth set in the Properties pane
  before drawing (remembered, and editable later); **Cutout**, which cuts openings out of a
  selected Area or Volume, with the value net and each cutout listed.
- **Angle** by three clicks (the inside angle, in degrees to one decimal, no scale needed).
- **Radius** and **Diameter** by the centre and a point on the edge, or by three points on the
  curve, drawn as the arc or the whole circle.
- **Count**: each click places a symbol and its number ("Door 7"), in count groups with their
  own name, symbol (circle, square, triangle, diamond, cross, tick), colour and size. Numbers run
  across the document; deleting one leaves a gap, and Renumber closes the gaps.
- On **Home** and **Comment**, one **Measure** button starts the tool you used last; its arrow
  lists every tool.

## One measurement, one comment

- Each measurement is saved as one standard PDF comment (a Line, PolyLine, Polygon or Stamp)
  with its value drawn into its own appearance and written as its text, and the tool's name as
  its subject. Acrobat Reader, other desktop PDF editors and the PDF viewer in Chrome or Edge
  show it, select it, move it and delete it as one item. Only Triya PDF edits it as a
  measurement.
- Files measured with 1.1.0 show an **Update old measurements** bar: one click turns each
  distance and its separate text box into one measurement, in one Undo step.
- A **Label** goes in front of the value ("Slab S1: 42.50 m²"). Each tool remembers its own
  colour, line width, line ends and fill. Captions can be dragged.

## Measuring that keeps up with you

- **Zoom, scroll and pan while you measure**: the points you placed stay, and the next click
  carries on. Only Esc, another tool or another page ends a measurement.
- **Shift** keeps the next point horizontal, vertical or at 45°, and still snaps where that
  direction meets a line of the drawing. Alt still means no snap for one click.
- **Change a placed measurement**: drag any point (the value follows live, with snapping, Shift
  and the loupe), drag a length's dimension line, right-click an edge to add a point or a point
  to remove it, and move points with the arrow keys. Each change is one Undo step.
- **A page with no scale** asks once: Set scale, Calibrate, or Measure at paper size.
- **Two scale areas**: while you draw, the value turns into a warning ("Crosses Detail A") when
  the pointer leaves the area you started in. A placed one has no value and is left out of the
  totals.

## Scales and viewports

- **Scale viewports on the page**: on the Measure tab, click a viewport's border to select it,
  drag to move it, use its handles to resize it, double-click or press Enter to change its name
  and scale, Delete to remove it, and the arrow keys to move it.
- **Measurements follow scale changes**: a new scale, a recalibration or a viewport change
  updates every measurement that uses it, in one Undo step, with a note.
- **Areas and volumes have their own units** and decimals: Automatic gives m² and m³ on a metric
  scale, sq ft and cu ft on an imperial one (cu yd on request), mm² and mm³ at paper size.

## The Measurements pane and export

- A new **Measurements** pane in the left rail lists every measurement, grouped by tool and by
  count group, with a total for each group, for this page or all pages. Click a row to show and
  select it. Flags mark measurements that span two scales or were measured at paper size.
- Its **Scales** section lists every page's scale and viewports (it replaces the viewport list
  in the Properties pane).
- **Export…** writes an Excel workbook (a Measurements sheet with real numbers and a Totals
  sheet), with an optional picture of each measurement, or a CSV file. **Attach to this PDF**
  puts the workbook into the document; the pane says when it is out of date, with Refresh.
- **Place summary** puts a table of the totals on the sheet, kept up to date as measurements
  change.
- Measurements stay in the Comments pane, with a **Measurements** filter there, and Export CRS
  leaves them out unless you choose **Include measurements**.
- **Same-Size Count Numbers** in the status bar keeps count numbers readable on screen at any
  zoom (screen only; off at first).

## Also new

- Help has new topics for scale viewports, volume and cutouts, angle, radius and diameter,
  counting, changing a measurement, the Measurements pane, exporting, and measurements in other
  PDF viewers, and its measuring pictures are new.
- The keyboard shortcut sheet lists Shift for measuring.
