**B15 fix.** While a text comment is edited, its editor shows all of its text: it grows past
its box instead of clipping, and a typewriter's lines don't wrap. When the edit is written
(Esc, a click elsewhere, any other way out), the comment grows to fit in the same undo step as
the text: a text box and a callout get taller (a callout's box grows down, its line stays and
the rect and `/RD` follow), a typewriter gets wider and taller; 2 pt is left below the last line
and 4 pt after a typewriter's longest line, so PDFium's own layout doesn't wrap it. They never
shrink by themselves, stay on the page, and a turned comment is left as it is
(`src/features/annotate/textEdit.ts`, `textEditModel.ts`).

Found and fixed during M7-12:

- **A text box with its own text colour had no text in strict readers** (ADR-024; Acrobat
  Reader likely, poppler and pdf.js certainly). Its recoloured appearance was set through
  `EPDFAnnot_SetAppearanceFromPage`, which makes the appearance's content a copy of the page's
  but puts only a wrapper form (`/EPDFWRAP`) in its `/Resources`. The page drew the content
  inline, so the appearance named `/Helv` and `/GS` without having them: PDFium drew it with its
  stock Helvetica, so the app and the pixel tests looked right. Now the page draws the recoloured
  form under the name `/EPDFWRAP` (the placement moves into the form's `/Matrix`), so the
  appearance's content finds the wrapper, and the wrapper the form with its fonts. New unit check
  `missingResources` in `tests/annotate/freetext-colors.test.ts` (every font, graphics state and
  XObject an appearance uses is in its own resources); placement still matches PDFium's to 1 px,
  turned or not. Callouts were not affected (PDFium draws their text colour itself).
- **B15** (section 2).

## 4. Known limits carried forward

- **Text comments:** one style for the whole comment (no rich text `/RC`); a text comment grows
  but never shrinks by itself; a turned text comment does not grow.
- **Sticky note pop-ups:** only sticky notes have a window; Acrobat and PDF-XChange may place a
  window they open for the first time by their own rules (T1–T4 check ours).
- **Comment rotation and vertical alignment** are still EmbedPDF-private keys (M6 limit).
- **Typewriter font:** the standard Courier, not Courier Prime (Q4; spike S24 only if wanted).
- **Install budget:** 2.4 MB of headroom.
- The M6 limits (forms, passwords, visual signatures) are unchanged ([M6 acceptance](M6.md) §3).
