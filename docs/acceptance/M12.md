## 3. Found and fixed during M12-23

- **A text comparison showed its first result only at the end** (D-66: "the first sheet or page
  as soon as it is done"). On the 500-page contract the first change appeared after 12 s here and
  20.5 s on CI, with the whole comparison done 1.8 s later. The early matches on the first 1, 2,
  4 … pages were queued in a later phase than the page reads, so they waited for every page of
  both documents. They now run as soon as their own pages are read (`textTasks` in
  `src/features/compare/engine/session.ts`): the first changes show after **about 1 s**, and the
  whole comparison takes as long as before. Test: "an early result does not wait for the last
  pages to be read" in `tests/compare/text-corpus.test.ts` (fails without the fix).
- **Rotated text was read with the wrong direction.** PDFium gives a character's angle clockwise
  and the material read it as counter-clockwise. Text reading up a sheet came out as reading down,
  and every line of a page stored with /Rotate 90 as running right to left. On pair 04's
  /Rotate 90 sheet every word of B came apart letter by letter, so the title block was reported as
  14 false text changes ("DETAIL → D E T A I L"); a text comparison would have left such a page out
  as rotated text. Fixed in `textOf` (`src/workers/work/compareMaterial.ts`); pair 04 now reports
  only the bedding depth, the date and the revision. Tests: the pair 04 case in
  `detect-corpus.test.ts` (fails without the fix) and two in `tests/compare/material-pdfium.test.ts`.
